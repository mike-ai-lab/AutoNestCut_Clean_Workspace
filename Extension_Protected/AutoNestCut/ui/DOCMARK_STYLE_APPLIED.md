1. **Premium Typography**: Plus Jakarta Sans with proper weights
2. **Consistent Blue Accent**: #2563eb used throughout for emphasis
3. **Generous Spacing**: More padding and margins for breathing room

5. **Visual Hierarchy**: Clear distinction between elements

7. **Accessibility**: Good contrast ratios and readable sizes
